# Structure of the game

## The underlying model of Epic Fantasy

1. World building
  - NPC creation procedures (small corner of the world, antagonist, etc.)
  - Unlock questions
  - GM World building questions (setting the scene)
  - GM moves (show how they are complacent, make it a false victory)
2. Culture and politics
  - NPC creation procedures
  - GM moves
  - World building questions
  - Unlock qeustions
3. Long character arcs
  - NPC Antagonists
  - Unlock questions
  - GM Moves
4. Magic
  - Magic move unlock questions
5. Multiple perspectives (and the conflict among them)
  - Allignment
  - Moves that incite personal cooperation and conflict
    - observe and violate allignment
